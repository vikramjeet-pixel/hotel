Garden Dining Room & Brasserie

Step into our award-winning Garden Restaurant and discover a dining experience that celebrates the very best of British seasonal produce. Our head chef, trained at some of London's finest establishments, crafts menus that change with the seasons — drawing inspiration from our own walled kitchen garden and trusted local suppliers.

The dining room itself is a masterpiece of Tudor architecture — exposed oak beams, stone mullioned windows, and candlelight that dances off centuries-old walls. Whether you're joining us for a leisurely Sunday lunch or a celebratory dinner, every visit is a memory in the making.

Bar & Lounge

The Twisted Boot Bar

Named after the ancient twisted boot found during the hotel's restoration, The Twisted Boot Bar is the beating heart of Kings Court. Settle into a leather Chesterfield beside the inglenook fireplace, order a pint of our house-brewed Golden Ale, and let the world slow down.

Our bar menu celebrates the best of British pub culture — elevated. From hand-crafted cocktails using local botanicals to a carefully curated whisky collection, there's something for every palate. Light bites are available throughout the day, perfect for a relaxed afternoon or a pre-dinner drink.
