Dining Room & Brasserie

Step into our award-winning Dining Room and discover an experience that celebrates the very best of British seasonal produce. Our head chef crafts menus that change with the seasons — drawing inspiration from our own walled kitchen garden and trusted local suppliers.

The Dining Room is a masterpiece of Tudor architecture — exposed oak beams, stone mullioned windows, and candlelight that dances off centuries-old walls. Whether you're joining us for a traditional Sunday roast or a celebratory dinner, every visit is a memory in the making.

Please note: The Garden Room at Kings Court is a private function and events space, not a public dining room. It is available exclusively for private hire, weddings, and corporate events.

Bar & Lounge

The Twisted Boot

Named after the ancient twisted boot found during the hotel's restoration, The Twisted Boot is the beating heart of Kings Court. Settle into a leather Chesterfield beside the inglenook fireplace, order a pint of our local Golden Ale, and let the world slow down.

Our bar menu celebrates the best of British pub culture — elevated. From hand-crafted cocktails using local botanicals to a carefully curated whisky collection, there's something for every palate. Light bites are available throughout the day, perfect for a relaxed afternoon or a pre-dinner drink.

Afternoon Tea

A Grand Tradition

Experience the timeless elegance of afternoon tea at Kings Court. Delight in delicate finger sandwiches, freshly baked scones with clotted cream and jam, and a selection of exquisite miniature cakes crafted by our pastry chef.

Choose from our tiered packages to suit your occasion, ranging from a simple Cream Tea to out fully indulgent Signature Afternoon Tea. Served daily, this charming tradition provides the perfect excuse to gather with friends or family in beautiful Tudor surroundings.
