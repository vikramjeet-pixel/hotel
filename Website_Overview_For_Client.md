# Kings Court Hotel - Website Pages Overview

This document provides a simple and understandable breakdown of every page on the Kings Court Hotel website and what information or features each page contains.

## Core Pages

**1. Home Page (`index.html`)**
The main landing page of the website. It gives visitors a complete overview of the hotel.
*   **Hero Video & Booking Widget:** A welcoming video background with a quick tool to check room availability.
*   **Welcome Section:** A brief introduction to the hotel's luxury and comfort.
*   **Room Showcase:** Highlights of the top room categories.
*   **Dining & Bar Highlights:** Quick links to the restaurant and the Twisted Boot Bar.
*   **Promotional Popup:** A special announcement popup (e.g., FIFA 2026 Live Screening) that appears when guests visit the site.
*   **Footer:** Quick links, newsletter signup, and contact details.

Tells the story of the Kings Court Hotel.
*   **History & Heritage:** Information about the hotel's background and values.
*   **Our Team:** Introductions to the management and hospitality team.
*   **Why Choose Us:** Key reasons for guests to stay at the hotel.

**3. Contact Us (`contact.html`)**
The primary page for getting in touch with the hotel.
*   **Contact Information:** The hotel's physical address, phone number, and email.
*   **Inquiry Form:** A simple form for guests to send direct messages or questions.
*   **Interactive Map:** A map showing the hotel's exact location.

## Accommodation & Booking

**4. Rooms & Suites (`rooms.html`)**
Displays all the different types of rooms available at the hotel.
*   **Room Listings:** Details for each category (e.g., Standard, Executive, Suites).
*   **Features & Pricing:** Starting prices and key amenities for each room type.
*   **Booking Links:** Buttons to easily check availability and book.

**5. Room Details (`room-detail.html`)**
A deep dive into a specific room type.
*   **Photo Gallery:** High-quality images of the specific room.
*   **Full Amenities List:** Everything included in the room (Wi-Fi, TV, bathroom features, etc.).
*   **Direct Booking:** A form to book that exact room type immediately.

**6. Booking Page (`booking.html`)**
The central reservation system page.
*   **Availability Checker:** A tool to select check-in and check-out dates.
*   **Room Selection:** The final step for guests to finalize their stay.

## Dining & Entertainment

**7. Restaurant (`dining.html`)**
Showcases the main dining experience.
*   **Culinary Philosophy:** Information about the chefs and the ingredients used.
*   **Menus:** Breakfast, lunch, and dinner options.
*   **Table Reservations:** A way for guests to book a table for their meal.

**8. Twisted Boot Bar (`twisted-boot-bar.html`)**
A dedicated page for the hotel's signature bar.
*   **Bar Atmosphere:** A premium book-like photo album showcasing the bar's environment.
*   **Christmas Menu:** A beautifully designed, interactive magazine-style menu for special seasonal offerings.
*   **Drinks & Snacks:** Information on cocktails, beers, and bar food.

## Events & Business

**9. Weddings (`weddings.html`)**
Everything a couple needs to know about hosting their special day at the hotel.
*   **Wedding Packages:** Details on different tiers of wedding services.
*   **Venues & Catering:** Photos and details of the event spaces and food options.
*   **Wedding Inquiry Form:** A specialized contact form for couples to start planning.

**10. Conferences & Meetings (`conferences.html`)**
Information for corporate guests and event organizers.
*   **Meeting Spaces:** Capacities, layouts, and equipment available (projectors, Wi-Fi, etc.).
*   **Corporate Packages:** Half-day and full-day delegate rates.

**11. Special Events (`events.html`)**
A calendar or listing of what's happening at the hotel.
*   **Upcoming Activities:** Seasonal parties, live music, or special gatherings (like the FIFA 2026 screenings).

## Guest Information & Extras

*   **Parking Information:** Details on the hotel's EV charging stations and parking facilities.
*   **Local Attractions:** Things to do and see nearby.

**13. Photo Gallery (`gallery.html`)**
A visual tour of the entire property.
*   **Albums:** Categorized photos of the exterior, lobby, rooms, dining, and event spaces.
