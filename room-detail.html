About This Room

Comfortable & Welcoming

Enjoy a comfortable stay in our individually styled en-suite bedroom, set within the stunning surroundings of the Warwickshire countryside. Each room features a pocket sprung luxury mattress, flatscreen TV with Freeview, and complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi.

Tea and coffee making facilities are provided in every room, along with a hairdryer and all the essentials for a relaxing stay. Guests also enjoy free on-site parking and complimentary access to our fitness centre.
