Your Stay

Beautiful, comfortable rooms designed for your needs

A Sanctuary in Warwickshire

Travelling for business or pleasure?

Located in Kings Coughton between Studley and Alcester, we offer a beautiful countryside retreat near Stratford upon Avon and the Cotswolds. The Kings Court Hotel ensures a visit worth remembering.

We feature 61 beautifully styled bedrooms alongside versatile accommodation. Whether you are arriving for sightseeing, business meetings, reunions, or a memorable wedding, we have elegant rooms suited for any occasion.

Perfectly Positioned

Explore the Local Splendor

Near to the M42 and within commuting distance to Birmingham, we are perfectly positioned for your travels. Enjoy close proximity to Worcester, Redditch, Cheltenham, and the historic Warwick Castle.

We are also proud to be one of the closest hotels to Ragley Hall — a spectacular stately home with 450 acres of parkland and gardens. Enjoy our warm hospitality while attending one of the many fairs and events hosted there throughout the year.
