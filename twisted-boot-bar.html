A Fantastic Selection of Food & Drink

Our Twisted Boot Bar (the Pub at the Kings Court Hotel) serves a fantastic selection of food and drink throughout the day until 11.00pm.

Looking for a place to pop in for a glass of wine or a pint after work? The Twisted Boot Bar is separate to the main hotel and boasts a large outdoor seating area, a great selection of beers, lagers and ciders, as well as a fabulous wine selection. Our friendly team looks forward to welcoming you!

Casual Comfort

Bar & Restaurant Dining

Prefer dining in more informal surroundings? Our full Kings Court Hotel menus can be served in our popular, relaxed Twisted Boot Bar for casual and comfortable dining.

Relax by our roaring log fire in winter or in our tranquil courtyard area during the spring and summer months. It's a great place to meet up for a pint, a coffee, a catch-up with friends, or a relaxed lunch in our beautiful location in Kings Coughton, Alcester.

Great Menu Choices

Twisted Boot has an excellent bar menu, with many beers, ales and an extensive Wine List. All with a great selection of vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options.
