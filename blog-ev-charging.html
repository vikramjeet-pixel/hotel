Stay at a Hotel with EV Charging On Site

December 2024 4 min read Sustainable Travel

As electric vehicles become increasingly popular, the way we travel is evolving. For many drivers, finding reliable charging stations along their route can be a source of anxiety. That's why choosing a hotel with EV charging on-site is becoming a top priority for both business and leisure travellers. At Kings Court Hotel, we understand this shift and are proud to offer convenient EV charging facilities for our guests.

The Convenience of On-Site EV Charging

Imagine arriving after a long drive, plugging in your vehicle, and heading straight to a comfortable room or our relaxed dining restaurant. With an EV charging hotel, there's no need to detour to a public charging station, wait in line, or sit in your car while it charges. You can simply park, plug in, and let your car recharge overnight while you do the same.

Sustainable Travel in Warwickshire

At Kings Court Hotel, we are committed to supporting sustainable travel. By providing an accommodation with EV charger, we hope to encourage more eco-friendly journeys to Stratford-upon-Avon and the beautiful Warwickshire countryside. Whether you are exploring Shakespeare's birthplace or attending a meeting, you can travel with peace of mind knowing your vehicle is powered by a reduced carbon footprint.

"Choosing a hotel with on-site EV chargers takes the stress out of eco-friendly travel. You wake up fully charged and ready for the day ahead."

Ideal for Business & Leisure Stays

For business travellers, time is money. An on-site EV charging facility means you don't waste valuable hours tracking down a rapid charger before your morning meeting. You can wake up, enjoy a hearty breakfast, and hit the road fully charged.

For leisure guests, it means your holiday time is spent enjoying the local attractions, our private walled gardens, or a drink at the Twisted Boot Bar, rather than monitoring a charging app in a supermarket car park.

Our Charging Facilities

We offer dedicated EV charging spaces located conveniently in our spacious, free car park. To use our chargers, simply speak to our reception team upon arrival. We recommend letting us know at the time of booking so we can aim to reserve a spot for you during busy periods.
