"
Every stone of Kings Court has witnessed centuries of celebration. Now it waits to witness yours.
— The Kings Court Wedding Team

Nestled within 4 acres of rolling Warwickshire countryside, Kings Court Hotel is more than a wedding venue — it is a living piece of history. Our 16th-century Tudor manor house, with its honey-stone façade, mullioned windows, and ancient oak beams, provides a backdrop of unparalleled romance. From the moment you arrive, our dedicated wedding team becomes yours — guiding every detail, anticipating every wish, ensuring your day unfolds exactly as you dreamed.
